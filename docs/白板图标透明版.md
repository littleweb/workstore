# 白板图标透明版

通过内置 image_gen 编辑，移除浅绿色底板，保留画板与绘图笔。资源：`public/icons/whiteboard-v3.png`，原 v2 保留。

## 最终提示词

Edit this WorkStore whiteboard application icon. Remove the entire pale sage green rounded square background tile, all its gradient/shading, and all shadows. Output a genuine transparent RGBA PNG with alpha=0 everywhere outside the foreground whiteboard and pencil, including between its two feet and around the pencil. Preserve the existing recognizable teal outline whiteboard, white drawing surface, teal freehand stroke, and diagonal teal pencil. No new shapes, no text. Keep the icon flat and crisp. Enlarge and center the remaining board-and-pencil foreground so its bounding box occupies approximately 94% of the image width, with only minimal transparent padding, for clear display at 20px in a navigation list. Do not render a checkerboard, solid color background, or any container tile. Transparent background is essential.
